# The four systemic gaps — 5 Nov 2026

From the BRJP26100123 proposal, in plain words:

1. **No visibility into conclusions.** Users cannot see how a conclusion was formed, so they over-trust or under-trust the agent. Fix: show the reasoning trace, not just the answer.

2. **Agents overstep.** An agent can misinterpret an instruction, exceed its permissions, or open a vulnerability when plugged into live systems. Fix: permission checks and plan validation *before* execution.

3. **Broad data access, no safeguards.** Reasoning needs lots of data, which can expose or misuse sensitive information. Fix: data-minimisation constraints in the plan, checked automatically.

4. **No accountability.** Without documented reasoning and approval checkpoints, no one can say who is responsible for a harmful outcome. Fix: audit log — plan version, human decision, and every executed step, timestamped.

Multimodal makes all four worse: visual and textual reasoning interact, so a decision can hinge on an image the human never saw explained.

This is why Week 1 starts with the plan, not the model.
